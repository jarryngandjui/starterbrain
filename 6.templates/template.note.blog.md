### 💭 The Spark
---
What sparked this? A conversation, a frustration, something you learned the hard way.

* **The idea in one sentence:**
* **Who is this for?** *(who needs to hear this, even if it's just future-you)*
* **Why now?** *(what makes this worth writing today, not "someday")*

### ✍️ Free Write
---
**Critical: no editing here. Set a timer and write until it goes off.**

Do not stop to fix a sentence. Do not reread a paragraph. Do not second-guess the opening line. Get the whole thought out of your head first, edit it second. A messy draft beats a blank page every time.

*(write below this line, unfiltered)*



### 🧵 Find the Line
---
Read what you wrote once, all the way through. Don't touch it yet.

* **What's the actual point buried in there?**
* **What's the strongest line you wrote?** *(that might be your opening or your closer)*
* **What can go?** *(tangents, throat-clearing, anything that isn't the point)*

### 🪓 Edit Pass
---
Now, and only now, tighten it.

- [ ] Cut the first paragraph if it's just warming up
- [ ] Read it out loud, fix what trips your tongue
- [ ] Kill filler words and hedges ("I think," "kind of," "just")
- [ ] Make sure the first line earns the second line
- [ ] Trim 10 to 20 percent. Shorter is almost always better.
