> [!tip] Progression Rule (so you are not guessing)
> **Strength lifts:** hit the top of the rep range with clean form on *all* sets, then add load next week. Miss it, repeat the load.
> **Jumps:** quality before quantity. Stick every landing and keep ground contact short before chasing height or load.
> **Re-test every 3–4 weeks.** The numbers tell you if the block is working. If they are not moving, change the plan, not your effort.
